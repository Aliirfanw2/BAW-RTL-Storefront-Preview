I want you to take the CURRENT BAW homepage and elevate the visual design substantially.

Do NOT rebuild the website technically.
Do NOT change the backend.
Do NOT add new functionality.

This is a DESIGN AND UX REFINEMENT.

The current design has a good foundation, but it still feels somewhat like an AI-generated concept. I want it to feel like a real, professionally art-directed eCommerce brand.

Think of the quality level of a premium modern DTC / lifestyle eCommerce website.

The design should feel:

* Premium
* Editorial
* Calm
* Confident
* Modern
* Commercial
* Highly intentional

The first impression should be strong within 3 seconds.

---

## 1. CHANGE THE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Do NOT try to make the page impressive by adding more content.

Instead:

LESS CONTENT
BETTER CONTENT
MORE WHITESPACE
STRONGER TYPOGRAPHY
BETTER IMAGERY
CLEARER HIERARCHY

Every section should have ONE clear purpose.

If a section does not add meaningful value, simplify it.

Avoid:

* Too many decorative elements
* Too many badges
* Too many CTAs
* Excessive rounded cards
* Repetitive promotional messages
* Large empty areas without purpose
* Sections that all look visually identical

---

## 2. HERO — MAKE THIS EXCEPTIONAL

The hero should be the strongest part of the entire website.

Use a sophisticated split layout:

IMAGE + BRAND MESSAGE

The product/lifestyle image should feel like a real campaign photograph.

Improve:

* Image scale
* Image cropping
* Image quality
* Typography
* Spacing
* CTA placement

Use a very large, confident headline.

Keep only:

* Small eyebrow
* Large headline
* Short supporting text
* One primary CTA
* One secondary CTA

Do not put too many badges or floating elements over the hero image.

The product promotion can remain, but make it subtle and elegant.

The hero should feel closer to a premium lifestyle brand than a discount marketplace.

---

## 3. HEADER — MINIMAL AND EXPENSIVE

Redesign the header to feel extremely clean.

Prioritize:

* BAW logo
* Search
* Main navigation
* Wishlist
* Account
* Cart

Reduce visual noise.

Use subtle borders rather than heavy boxes.

Make the header feel light and sophisticated.

Keep the RTL layout.

---

## 4. CATEGORY DISCOVERY — SIMPLIFY IT

The current pastel category cards are too dominant.

Make the category section more elegant.

Instead of large dashboard-like cards, create a refined category navigation system.

Use:

* Smaller cards
* Better typography
* Simple icons
* More whitespace
* Consistent proportions

The user should feel like they are browsing a curated store, not an admin dashboard.

Keep the pastel BAW palette but use it more subtly.

---

## 5. PRODUCT DISCOVERY — MAKE PRODUCTS THE HERO

The products should be visually more important than the decorative UI.

Create a clean editorial product grid.

Use fewer products per row with larger, better images.

Each card should have:

Product image
Wishlist
Small badge when necessary
Product name
Rating
Price
Previous price
Discount
Add to cart

Do not put every possible label on every card.

Only show badges when they communicate something meaningful.

Use strong product photography and generous whitespace.

---

## 6. FEATURED PRODUCT

Keep ONE featured product story.

Make it feel like a campaign/editorial section.

Large lifestyle image on one side.

Short product story on the other.

Use:

* Small eyebrow
* Strong headline
* 2–3 lines of supporting copy
* Price
* One CTA

Do not overload this section.

This should feel like a premium product advertisement.

---

## 7. PROMOTIONAL SECTION

Keep the "4 for 3" promotion because it is commercially important.

But redesign it as ONE strong campaign banner.

Do not make it look like a generic colored rectangle.

Use:

* Large lifestyle/product image
* Strong headline
* Short explanation
* One CTA

The promotion should feel like part of the brand, not an advertisement inserted into the page.

---

## 8. TRUST SECTION

Keep:

Safe purchase
14-day returns
Cash payment
Home delivery

But make this section extremely subtle.

Use a clean horizontal benefits bar with small icons.

It should reassure the customer without becoming a major visual section.

---

## 9. FOOTER

Make the footer cleaner and more premium.

Use:

* BAW logo
* Short brand statement
* Customer service
* Shopping links
* Newsletter
* Social links
* Legal links

Use generous spacing.

Avoid making the footer visually heavy.

---

# VISUAL SYSTEM

Use the existing BAW palette:

Deep teal
Warm cream
Coral orange
Soft muted pastels

But reduce the amount of coral.

Coral should be an accent color used for:

* Primary CTA
* Important promotion
* Small highlights

Do not use coral everywhere.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Typography should feel editorial and premium.

Create strong contrast between:

Eyebrow
Large headline
Supporting text
Product information

Use fewer font sizes but make the hierarchy much stronger.

Avoid excessive bold text.

---

# SPACING

This is extremely important.

Use generous, intentional spacing.

Every section should have breathing room.

But do NOT create huge blank spaces.

The page should feel spacious, not empty.

---

# MOTION

Use subtle premium interactions:

* Smooth image hover
* Gentle product card lift
* Button hover
* Image transitions
* Subtle navigation interactions

No excessive animations.

No flashy effects.

---

# MOBILE

Do NOT simply shrink the desktop layout.

Design the mobile experience intentionally.

On mobile:

* Compact premium header
* Strong hero
* Horizontal category navigation
* 2-column product grid where appropriate
* Large product imagery
* Clear CTA
* Comfortable spacing
* No horizontal overflow

---

# FINAL ART DIRECTION

The final homepage should feel like:

"A premium Israeli lifestyle marketplace that happens to have great prices."

NOT:

"An AI-generated ecommerce template."

Use inspiration from high-quality modern editorial eCommerce design, but DO NOT copy another website.

Create an original BAW design language.

The final page should make the client immediately think:

"This looks like a real professional redesign."

Before finishing, remove anything that feels unnecessary, repetitive, generic or visually noisy.

Prioritize QUALITY over the amount of content.
