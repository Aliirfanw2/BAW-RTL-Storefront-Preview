Focus ONLY on perfecting the BAW HOMEPAGE for the client preview.

Do not add new sections just to make the page longer. Do not make the homepage crowded.

The most important goal is:

When the client opens the homepage for the FIRST TIME, it should immediately feel PREMIUM, MODERN, CLEAN, TRUSTWORTHY and professionally designed.

### PREMIUM FIRST-GLANCE PRINCIPLE

Prioritize quality over quantity.

Every section must have a clear purpose.

Remove, simplify, or merge anything that feels repetitive or unnecessary.

The page should have generous breathing room, strong visual hierarchy and intentional spacing.

Do NOT try to show everything at once.

### HERO — MOST IMPORTANT

Make the hero the strongest visual moment on the page.

It should immediately communicate:

* BAW brand
* Useful products
* Attractive prices
* Premium shopping experience

Improve:

* Typography hierarchy
* Headline scale
* Image quality and composition
* CTA hierarchy
* Promotional badge
* Spacing
* Visual balance

The hero should feel editorial and premium, not like a generic AI-generated ecommerce template.

Do not overcrowd the hero with too many buttons, labels or messages.

Keep ONE clear primary CTA and ONE secondary CTA.

### HEADER

Make the header extremely clean.

Prioritize:

* BAW logo
* Search
* Main navigation
* Wishlist
* Account
* Cart

Avoid unnecessary elements.

The header should feel like a premium retail website.

### CATEGORIES

Keep the category section visually simple.

Do not display too many categories in a way that creates visual noise.

Use consistent cards, excellent spacing and simple icons.

The category section should help users discover products without competing with the hero.

### PRODUCT DISCOVERY

Keep a strong bestseller/product section, but avoid showing too many products.

Use a focused product row with high-quality cards.

Every card should have excellent:

* Image
* Product name
* Rating
* Price
* Discount
* Wishlist
* Add to cart

Prioritize product image quality and whitespace.

### PROMOTIONAL CONTENT

Keep only the strongest promotional concepts.

Do not create multiple visually competing banners.

Use large, beautiful imagery with short messaging and one clear CTA.

The promotions should feel like a premium campaign, not advertisements stacked on top of each other.

### TRUST ELEMENTS

Keep the important trust signals, but present them subtly.

They should support conversion without taking attention away from products.

### VISUAL RHYTHM

Create a strong rhythm throughout the page:

Premium Hero
↓
Simple Product Discovery
↓
Categories
↓
Focused Bestseller Products
↓
One Strong Promotional Moment
↓
Trust
↓
Clean Footer

Do not make every section visually heavy.

Some sections should be quiet so the important sections can stand out.

### SPACING

Use generous whitespace.

Avoid:

* Sections touching each other
* Huge empty areas with no purpose
* Crowded text
* Too many cards
* Too many colors
* Too many CTAs

Every section should have comfortable breathing room.

### COLOR

Keep the existing BAW palette:

* Deep teal
* Warm cream/beige
* Coral/orange

Use coral as an accent, NOT everywhere.

The page should feel sophisticated rather than colorful for the sake of being colorful.

### TYPOGRAPHY

Create a strong typography hierarchy:

Small eyebrow
↓
Large headline
↓
Short supporting text
↓
Clear CTA

Use consistent heading sizes and line heights throughout the page.

### RESPONSIVE

The premium feeling must remain on mobile.

Do not simply shrink the desktop layout.

Recompose sections specifically for mobile:

* Compact header
* Strong hero
* Horizontal category/product scrolling where appropriate
* Comfortable spacing
* Clear CTAs
* No horizontal overflow

### FINAL DESIGN REVIEW

Before finishing, review the homepage at desktop and mobile.

Ask:

"Does this look premium within the first 3 seconds?"

"Is anything unnecessary?"

"Is the page too crowded?"

"Does anything look like an AI-generated template?"

"Is the user's eye naturally guided from hero → discovery → products → purchase?"
